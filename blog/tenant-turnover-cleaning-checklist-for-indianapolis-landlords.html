Tenant Turnover Cleaning Checklist for Indianapolis Landlords

What to check outside a rental property between tenants — not just inside.

Turnover checklists tend to focus heavily on the interior — paint touch-ups, deep cleaning, appliance checks — while the exterior gets a quick glance at best. That's a missed opportunity, both for protecting the property and for the listing photos that attract your next tenant.

Why Exterior Condition Affects Your Next Tenant Search

Just like a home sale, rental listing photos are doing the work of getting a prospective tenant to schedule a viewing at all. A grimy exterior in listing photos undersells a property that might otherwise show well, and can even suggest to prospective tenants that maintenance requests won't be taken seriously once they move in.

Exterior Turnover Checklist

  • Siding: check for algae streaking or general grime, especially if the previous tenancy was longer than a year without a cleaning
  • Driveway and walkways: oil stains from tenant vehicles are common and worth addressing before new photos
  • Gutters: often neglected during a tenancy; check for clogs or overflow staining on siding below the gutter line
  • Porch, entryway, and any patio/deck: these show prominently in listing photos and get disproportionate attention from prospective tenants during a walkthrough
  • Trash/dumpster area (multi-unit properties): odor and pest concerns compound if this is skipped between tenants

Timing It Right

Scheduling exterior cleaning to align with your interior turnover work means the property is genuinely ready — inside and out — by the time new listing photos are taken, rather than photographing a clean interior against a grimy exterior that undercuts the overall impression.

Protecting the Property, Not Just the Listing

Turnover is also a natural checkpoint to catch issues before they compound under a new tenancy — a clogged gutter or algae-streaked siding left unaddressed through one tenancy only gets worse through the next one.
