How Much Does Pressure Washing Cost in Indianapolis? (2026 Price Guide)

What Indianapolis homeowners actually pay for pressure washing in 2026, broken down by job type and the factors that move the price.

If you've searched "pressure washing cost Indianapolis" and gotten ten different answers, that's because the honest answer really is "it depends." But it doesn't have to be a mystery. After pricing hundreds of jobs across Marion County and the surrounding suburbs, we can give you real ranges instead of a vague "call for pricing."

Typical Indianapolis Pressure Washing Prices by Job

Most single-service jobs in the Indianapolis area fall into these ranges:

  • House/siding washing: $250–$450 for an average 2,000–2,800 sq ft home
  • Driveway or concrete cleaning: $150–$300 depending on square footage and staining
  • Roof soft washing: $300–$600 depending on roof pitch and size
  • Deck or fence cleaning: $180–$350
  • Gutter cleaning (exterior/interior): $150–$300
  • Commercial storefronts/lots: priced per square foot, typically $0.15–$0.35/sq ft

Bundling two or more services — house wash plus driveway, for example — almost always brings the combined price down compared to booking them separately, since we're already on-site with equipment set up.

What Actually Moves the Price

Square footage matters, but it's rarely the only factor. The bigger swing factors we see on Indianapolis properties are:

  • Story count and access: a two-story home with limited yard access takes longer and needs more hose/ladder setup than a single-story ranch.
  • Surface condition: years of built-up algae, rust stains, or oil stains on concrete take more time (and sometimes specialty chemicals) than routine annual maintenance.
  • Surface type: soft-washing delicate vinyl siding or a shingle roof requires lower pressure and specific detergents, which is a different process (and price) than blasting a concrete driveway.
  • How long since the last cleaning: homes that haven't been washed in 3+ years often need a second pass or pre-treatment for black streaking, which adds time.

Why "Per Square Foot" Isn't the Whole Story

Some companies quote a flat per-square-foot rate site-unseen. We'd rather give you an accurate number than a fast one. A quick photo or a walk-around of the property lets us spot things like heavy oxidation, overgrown landscaping we'll need to work around, or delicate surfaces that change the approach — all of which affect the real price more than square footage alone.

How to Avoid Surprise Charges

The biggest complaint homeowners have after a bad pressure washing experience isn't the price — it's a price that changed after the work started. Ask any company for a written estimate before they begin, and ask specifically whether the quote includes all surfaces you want cleaned or just some of them.
