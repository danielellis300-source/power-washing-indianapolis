Selling Your Home in Indianapolis? Why Power Washing Boosts Curb Appeal

The first thing buyers see is the exterior — here’s why cleaning it is one of the cheapest, highest-impact things you can do before listing.

Real estate agents throughout Indianapolis consistently list exterior cleaning as one of the highest-return, lowest-cost pre-listing preparations — well ahead of most cosmetic renovations, for a fraction of the price.

Buyers Decide Before They Get Out of the Car

First impressions in real estate happen fast, often within the first few seconds of pulling up to a listing. A home with visible algae streaking, a stained driveway, or dingy siding creates an immediate, often subconscious impression of neglect — even if the interior is immaculate. That impression is hard to fully undo once it's formed, regardless of what buyers see once they're inside.

Listing Photos Matter Even More Than the Showing

Most buyers filter listings online before ever requesting a showing, which means your exterior photos are doing the work of getting a buyer to click at all. A freshly washed home photographs noticeably better — cleaner sightlines, brighter siding color, and a driveway free of the tire-track staining and salt haze that shows up clearly in photos even when it's easy to overlook in person.

What to Prioritize Cleaning Before Listing

  • House exterior/siding: the single biggest visual impact for the cost
  • Driveway and front walkway: the path every buyer physically walks
  • Front porch and entryway: where buyers pause longest before going inside
  • Roof (if streaking is visible from the street): especially relevant if the roof is a selling point

The Cost-to-Impact Ratio

A full exterior cleaning package typically runs a few hundred dollars — a small fraction of what most pre-listing renovations cost, with a disproportionately large effect on how the home is perceived. It's consistently one of the best-ROI items on a pre-listing checklist, which is why agents recommend it so often. Timing It Around Your Listing Date

Schedule your exterior cleaning close to when listing photos will be taken — ideally within a week or two — rather than weeks in advance, since pollen, rain, and everyday grime can start dulling the results again fairly quickly, especially in spring.
