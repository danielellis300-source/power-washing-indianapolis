Best Time of Year to Pressure Wash Your Home in Indianapolis

Why timing your pressure washing around Indianapolis’s seasons gets you better, longer-lasting results.

Pressure washing can technically happen almost any time the temperature is above freezing, but "can" and "should" are different questions. Timing it right in Indianapolis's climate gets you better results and can extend how long your property stays clean.

Spring: The Most Popular Season, for Good Reason

Spring is when Indianapolis homes show the most visible grime — a winter's worth of road salt splashed onto lower siding and driveways, pollen coating every horizontal surface, and algae that's been quietly growing through damp, mild months finally becoming obvious as things warm up. Washing in April or May clears all of it at once and sets your property up clean before the peak-growth summer months. See our full spring exterior cleaning checklist.

Summer: Good for Algae, Tighter on Scheduling

Summer works well for soft washing since warm temperatures help the cleaning solution work effectively against algae and mildew. The tradeoff is that it's our busiest season, so booking a few weeks ahead helps guarantee your preferred date, especially heading into a holiday weekend when everyone wants their property looking sharp at once.

Fall: Underrated for Prevention

Cleaning in September or October removes the summer's accumulated algae and grime before it sits under fallen leaves and winter moisture for months. It also pairs naturally with fall gutter cleaning, since leaves are already down. A fall wash gives your home's exterior a head start before winter weather makes any kind of exterior cleaning impractical.

Winter: Possible, But Limited

Pressure washing in winter is possible above freezing temperatures, but Indianapolis's unpredictable cold snaps make scheduling unreliable, and there's real risk of water freezing on surfaces or in equipment lines. We go into more detail on this in can you pressure wash in the winter? — short version: it's rarely the ideal choice here.

The Case for an Annual Rhythm

Rather than reacting when your home visibly needs it, an annual wash — timed for spring or fall — keeps algae and mildew from ever getting a strong foothold in the first place. Homes we service on a yearly schedule throughout Plainfield and similar suburbs consistently need less aggressive treatment at each visit than homes that wait 2–3 years between cleanings, because we're never dealing with deeply set-in growth.

Booking Around Your Schedule

Whatever season works for your calendar, we offer flexible scheduling seven days a week. If you're not sure whether now is a good time given your home's specific situation, just ask — we're happy to give an honest recommendation, even if it means telling you to wait a few weeks.
