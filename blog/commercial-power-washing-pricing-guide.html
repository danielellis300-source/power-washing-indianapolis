Commercial Power Washing Pricing Guide for Indianapolis Businesses

How commercial power washing pricing works for Indianapolis storefronts, sidewalks, and parking lots.

Commercial pricing works differently than residential — it's almost always quoted per square foot rather than as a flat job price, because commercial properties vary so much in scale. Here's how it typically breaks down for Indianapolis businesses.

Typical Commercial Rates

  • Storefront/entryway washing: $0.20–$0.35/sq ft
  • Sidewalks and walkways: $0.15–$0.25/sq ft
  • Parking lots (surface cleaning): $0.10–$0.20/sq ft depending on size
  • Dumpster pad cleaning: $75–$175 flat, often billed as a recurring service

Larger jobs generally bring the per-square-foot rate down, similar to volume pricing in most trades.

One-Time Cleaning vs. Recurring Contracts

Many Indianapolis businesses — especially retail storefronts, restaurants, and office parks — move to a recurring schedule (monthly or quarterly) once they see how quickly grease, gum, algae, and general grime return in high-foot-traffic areas. Recurring contracts typically run a lower effective rate per visit than one-off cleanings, since scheduling and setup become more efficient over repeat visits.

What Drives Commercial Pricing Up

  • Gum and grease removal: heavily trafficked sidewalks near restaurants need pre-treatment, not just water pressure.
  • Oil-stained parking areas: similar to residential driveways, but at commercial scale, oil staining under regular parking spots needs degreasing agents.
  • After-hours scheduling: cleaning outside business hours to avoid disrupting customers or employees sometimes carries a modest premium.
  • Water access and drainage considerations: commercial properties have specific requirements for where wash water can go — see our guide on Indianapolis stormwater rules for why this matters more for commercial lots than a residential driveway.
