Driveway & Concrete Cleaning Cost in Indianapolis

Driveway and concrete cleaning pricing in Indianapolis, and why winter road salt makes spring the busiest season for this service.

Concrete cleaning is deceptively simple to price in theory — square footage times a rate — but in practice, what's actually on the concrete matters as much as how much of it there is. Here's what Indianapolis homeowners typically pay.

Typical Pricing

  • Standard driveway (single-car, ~400–600 sq ft): $120–$200
  • Double-wide or long driveway (600–1,200 sq ft): $180–$320
  • Driveway + walkway + porch combo: $220–$400
  • Patio cleaning: $130–$280 depending on size and furniture that needs moving

Why Winter Salt Makes This a Spring Rush

Indianapolis winters mean road salt and de-icing chemicals tracked onto driveways for months, plus the freeze-thaw cycle that pushes embedded dirt and mineral staining deeper into concrete pores. By March and April, driveways that looked fine in November often show white salt haze, dark tire-track staining, and moss creeping in along shaded edges. This is exactly why spring is our busiest season for concrete cleaning — see our spring exterior cleaning checklist for the full seasonal rundown.

Oil Stains and Rust Add to the Price

A driveway with a few years of dripped motor oil under where cars park, or rust rings from patio furniture, needs a degreasing pre-treatment before pressure washing — standard pressure alone won't fully lift set-in oil. Expect this to add $30–$75 depending on the size and severity of the stain. Rust from sprinkler heads or metal furniture responds to a different chemical treatment entirely, so mention any known stains when you request your quote.

Should You Seal It Afterward?

Sealing isn't required, but it's worth considering right after a professional cleaning while the surface is at its cleanest. A quality concrete sealer helps repel future oil stains, resists salt penetration next winter, and can extend the time between deep cleanings from one year to two. It's a separate service from washing itself, and we're happy to talk through whether it makes sense for your specific driveway.

DIY Rental Pressure Washers vs. Professional Equipment

A rented consumer-grade pressure washer can clean concrete, but professional equipment runs at higher, more consistent PSI and uses surface cleaner attachments that clean evenly without the classic "stripe" pattern DIY jobs often leave behind.
