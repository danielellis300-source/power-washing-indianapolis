Gutter Cleaning Cost in Indianapolis: What Homeowners Pay

What gutter cleaning costs in Indianapolis, and why skipping it causes more damage here than in drier regions.

Gutter cleaning is one of those maintenance items that's easy to put off — until a clogged downspout sends water straight down your foundation during a heavy spring storm. Here's what it typically costs to stay ahead of it in the Indianapolis area.

Typical Pricing

  • Single-story home: $120–$200
  • Two-story home: $180–$300
  • Heavy debris (mature trees, not cleaned in 2+ years): add $40–$100

Pricing is generally based on linear footage of gutter, story count (which affects ladder work and safety setup), and how much debris has actually built up.

Why Indianapolis Trees Make This Worse

Neighborhoods with mature oak, maple, and sycamore trees — common throughout Indianapolis and suburbs like Noblesville — mean gutters fill fast in the fall and can develop standing water and small plant growth by spring if left unaddressed. Standing water in clogged gutters also becomes a mosquito breeding ground during Indiana's humid summer months, which is an extra reason not to let it sit.

What Happens If You Skip It

Clogged gutters don't just look bad — they redirect water exactly where you don't want it. Overflow during heavy rain runs down siding (leading to the same streaking and algae growth we cover in our humidity and algae guide), pools at the foundation, and over time can contribute to basement moisture problems that are far more expensive to fix than a gutter cleaning ever costs.

How Often You Actually Need It

Most Indianapolis homes do well with cleaning twice a year: once in late spring after trees finish dropping seed pods and flower debris, and once in late fall after leaves are down. Homes without significant tree cover nearby can often get away with once a year. If you're not sure which category your property falls into, we're happy to take a look.

Bundling With Other Services

Gutter cleaning pairs naturally with a house wash or roof soft wash, since we're already on ladders and around the roofline. Bundling typically saves compared to booking gutter cleaning as a standalone visit later — worth asking about when you request your quote.
