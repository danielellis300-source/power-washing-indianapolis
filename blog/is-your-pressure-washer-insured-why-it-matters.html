Is Your Pressure Washing Company Insured? Why It Matters

What insurance actually protects you from on a pressure washing job — and what you’re exposed to without it.

"Are you insured?" is a simple question, but the answer matters more in pressure washing than most home service jobs, because the equipment involved is genuinely capable of causing damage fast — and the financial exposure without coverage falls on the homeowner, not just the company.

What Can Actually Go Wrong

High-pressure water is powerful enough to crack window seals, strip exterior paint, gouge wood decking, and force water into gaps in siding or window frames. Ladders and roof access for gutter or roof work carry their own injury risk. None of this is rare or exotic — it's the everyday risk profile of the job, which is exactly why insurance exists for this trade specifically.

What General Liability Insurance Covers

A general liability policy typically covers property damage caused by the company's work — a cracked window, damaged siding, ruined landscaping — and injury that occurs on your property during the job. Without it, you'd need to pursue the individual or company directly for damages, with no guarantee they have the assets to actually cover the cost.

Why "I've Never Had a Problem" Isn't the Same as Insured

Plenty of uninsured operators go years without an incident — until they don't. The point of insurance isn't that damage is likely on any given job; it's that when something does go wrong, you're not the one absorbing the cost of a mistake that wasn't yours. This is especially relevant on jobs involving your roof, windows, or delicate landscaping, where a small equipment slip can get expensive fast.
